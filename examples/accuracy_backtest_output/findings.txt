Kronos-small BTC/USDT Backtest Findings
=========================================
Run date: 2026-07-28
Model: NeoQuasar/Kronos-small (tokenizer: NeoQuasar/Kronos-Tokenizer-base)
Test window: ~6 months back from run date (TEST_DAYS=182)
Symbol: BTCUSDT (Binance public REST API)
Market regime note: BTC was in a downtrend over this window
  (buy & hold return was roughly -25% to -28% across all three granularities).

Scripts used:
  examples/backtest_btc_accuracy.py  -> walk-forward accuracy backtest
  examples/pnl_backtest.py           -> directional long/short P&L simulation on top of it

Takeaway: price-level correlation is high (model tracks the general level/trend

directional edge at the bar level, even though it fits the overall series shape.

2. P&L BACKTEST (simple long/short-per-bar strategy, $10,000 start)
---------------------------------------------------------
Strategy: at each bar, go long if Kronos predicted a higher close than the
last known real close, short if lower. Apply that bar's real return, minus
a slippage/fee cost on every position change.

Takeaway: the strategy underperformed plain buy & hold at every granularity
and slippage level tested. Underperformance is worst at higher trade
frequency (hourly, 15-min), where slippage compounds on top of a
directional edge that is statistically no better than a coin flip,
driving the account toward total loss. Daily is the least bad of the
three but still trails buy & hold.

---------------------------------------------------------
3. CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------------------------
As configured (naive long/short-every-bar on Kronos-small's directional
prediction, no confidence filtering, no position sizing, no trend/regime
filter), this is not a viable trading strategy. The ~50% directional
accuracy cannot absorb even 0.20% round-trip slippage once trade frequency
rises. Any future attempt should consider:
  - Filtering trades by prediction confidence/magnitude instead of trading
    every single bar
  - Position sizing or a minimum-move threshold before acting
  - Testing Kronos-base/large for comparison against Kronos-small
  - Testing across bull and sideways regimes, not just this downtrend window
  - Reducing trade frequency to cut slippage drag
