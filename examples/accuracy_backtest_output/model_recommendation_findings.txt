VERDICT
---------------------------------------------------------
The two 1-month snapshots disagree with each other on which model wins
(Base ahead in the down-trend month at daily/hourly, Small ahead in the
recent month at hourly/15-min) -- small samples, noisy, exactly what
you'd expect given the noise-floor analysis from the earlier
hyperparameter sweep. Neither 1-month window should be trusted alone.

The 6-month result is the one to weight most heavily -- it's by far the
largest sample of the three tests, and it's unambiguous: Kronos-small
wins (or ties) at every granularity, most decisively at hourly
(+42.7% vs. +22.5%, over 6x the gap seen at daily). Kronos-base is also
~4x the parameters and noticeably slower to run on this CPU-only setup,
for a result that's flat-out worse over the longest, most reliable test.

RECOMMENDATION: Use Kronos-small, not Kronos-base, paired with the
indicator filter + hysteresis strategy. Best result found across this
entire project: hourly, Filtered+Hysteresis, 0.20% slippage -> +42.73%
over 6 months, 58.8% win rate. Do note this collapses badly at 0.54%
slippage (hourly filtered: +42.73% -> -79.38%), so this result only
holds up with low-cost/low-slippage execution -- that's the binding
constraint on this strategy, not model choice.
