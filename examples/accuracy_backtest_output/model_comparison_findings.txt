Kronos-small vs. Kronos-base — Accuracy Comparison
=====================================================
Scripts: examples/backtest_btc_accuracy.py (run twice, once per model,
         via KRONOS_MODEL_NAME/KRONOS_OUT_SUBDIR env vars) +
         examples/compare_kronos_models.py
Test window: ~182 days (6 months) BTC/USDT, same walk-forward setup
             (context_len/block_len) as all prior runs.
Kronos-small results: examples/accuracy_backtest_output/btc_*_results.csv
Kronos-base results:  examples/accuracy_backtest_output/kronos-base/btc_*_results.csv

---------------------------------------------------------
RESULTS
---------------------------------------------------------
Model        | Granularity | MAE      | RMSE     | MAPE  | Dir.Acc | Corr
Kronos-base  | Daily       | 3483.347 | 4730.461 | 5.023 | 52.78%  | 0.728
Kronos-small | Daily       | 3261.489 | 4572.432 | 4.678 | 47.22%  | 0.765
Kronos-base  | Hourly      | 1017.573 | 1463.510 | 1.465 | 49.59%  | 0.974
Kronos-small | Hourly      | 1086.350 | 1509.700 | 1.554 | 49.76%  | 0.973
Kronos-base  | 15-Minute   | 1002.649 | 1477.225 | 1.439 | 50.60%  | 0.973
Kronos-small | 15-Minute   | 1113.132 | 1565.931 | 1.597 | 50.87%  | 0.971

Directional accuracy delta (base - small):
  Daily:      +5.56pp (base better)
  Hourly:     -0.17pp (essentially tied)
  15-Minute:  -0.27pp (essentially tied)

---------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Hourly and 15-minute: directional accuracy is statistically identical
   between the two model sizes (deltas well under 1pp). Given the
   hyperparameter-sweep finding (examples/accuracy_backtest_output/
   param_sweep_findings.txt) that ~4pp of spread is just sampling noise
   at this kind of sample size, a <0.3pp gap is not a real difference --
   Kronos-base (4x the params of Kronos-small) buys NOTHING on
   directional accuracy at these granularities.

2. Daily shows a +5.56pp gap in base's favor, which superficially looks
   more interesting, but it's right at the edge of the noise floor
   established by the sweep (~4.16pp for this sample size), AND the
   Kronos-small daily numbers here differ slightly from the very first
   findings.txt run (49.3% there vs. 47.2% here) purely because that
   file got regenerated once already with fresh unseeded sampling (see
   the caveat noted during the parameter-sweep work). With that much
   run-to-run wobble already documented on the small side alone, a
   single-run 5.56pp gap on daily is suggestive at best, not confirmed --
   would need repeated runs (like the sweep's reproducibility check) to
   trust it.

3. Error metrics (MAE/RMSE/MAPE) lean slightly in base's favor at hourly
   and 15-minute, slightly in small's favor at daily. None of the gaps
   are large relative to the price scale (BTC ~$60-80k over this
   period).

4. Cost/benefit: Kronos-base is meaningfully slower to run (4x the
   parameters of Kronos-small, same CPU-only environment) for a
   directional-accuracy improvement that doesn't clear the noise floor
   at 2 of 3 granularities and is unconfirmed at the third. Not a clear
   win.

---------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------------------------
Consistent with the earlier finding that the ~50% directional-accuracy
ceiling looks like a property of the model family/task on this data, not
something fixable by tuning: it also isn't fixed by moving to a larger
pretrained checkpoint. Kronos-base is not a clear upgrade over
Kronos-small for this specific goal (predicting next-bar BTC direction).
If pursuing model-size effects further, the daily result is the one
result worth re-checking with repeat runs before drawing conclusions --
everything else here is a tie.
